ABHYAZ · ZOHO LEARN MONETISATION DEMO

Objective & Proposed Approach: Course Monetisation & Enrolment Management

Objective: Abhyaz offers structured technical and Zoho courses and needed to monetise them while exploring end-to-end enrolment management and monetisation management across individual learners and college cohorts. Below is the full proposed approach connecting commerce checkout, CRM deals, Zoho Flow automation, Zoho Learn enrolment and completion, filtered college analytics, optional renewals, and an optional per-college portal.

Narration & Auto-Advance

Abhyaz sells technical and Zoho courses. We want one simple way to sell them, enrol learners, and report to colleges. Learners can try a free two-lesson preview first. Zoho Commerce then takes the payment. Zoho CRM keeps a record of every buyer. Zoho Flow gives the learner access automatically, and Zoho Analytics shows each college its own progress. A separate college portal is an optional extra.

1. Monetisation & UPI Checkout
Zoho Commerce Storefront

Learners browse CNC, Robotics, Pneumatics, and Zoho courses, register with their college at checkout, pay via UPI, and optionally renew for a follow-up term. Abhyaz assigns each college an ID for tracking.

2. Enrolment Management
CRM → Zoho Flow → Zoho Learn

Paid orders create CRM Contacts and Deals, trigger Zoho Flow to enrol learners in Zoho Learn, track course study, and mark deals Closed Won on completion.

3. College Reporting & Governance
Filtered Zoho Analytics

One College Progress dashboard shared per College ID with View + Export only (no Edit), with paid seats or scheduled reports per your Zoho plan.

4. Optional College Portal
Quoted Per College Add-On

Optional per-college learning portal with its own login, showing only the courses shared with that college. Portal users view content only; college managers see progress through Zoho Analytics or as internal users.

Experience Before You Buy: Free Course Preview Model

Separate 2-lesson preview courses on Zoho Learn let learners and colleges evaluate quality before purchasing the full course.

Narration

Before paying, people can try a free preview. Each course has a small separate preview course with two lessons: an introduction and a sample practical lesson. The full course stays locked until payment is confirmed. We use two separate courses because Zoho Learn cannot lock single lessons inside one course. This keeps the paid course safe, and the preview open to everyone.

Slide 08 · Optional Add-On Portal Screenshot

Optional Dedicated Portal (Additional Cost Per College)

Available as an optional add-on at an additional cost quoted per college—giving the college's learners their own learning space. Zoho confirmed portal users can only view the content shared with them, so college managers who need to see learner progress are added as internal users.

Narration

Some colleges may want their own portal. This is an optional extra, priced for each college. It gives the college's learners their own login, and shows only the courses shared with that college. Portal users can only view that content. They cannot see learner reports. So college managers see progress through the Zoho Analytics dashboard, or are added as internal users. The college never sees other colleges, cannot edit courses, and cannot see Abhyaz's CRM or billing.
